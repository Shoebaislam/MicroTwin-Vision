**MicroTwin-Vision — Physics-Aware Battery Electrode Microstructure Analysis**
*Independent Research Project | October 2026–Present | In preparation*

Building a lightweight, reproducible workflow to predict PDE-derived effective transport behavior from **synthetic** 2D lithium-ion battery electrode microstructures. The approach compares physically meaningful geometric features—porosity, connectivity, and interface density—with pixel-based PCA features using Ridge regression.

The planned evaluation covers MAE, R², controlled pixel-noise robustness, and engineering trade-offs between interpretability, predictive performance, and computational cost. Data source: Weber, Korneev & Battiato (2022), Mendeley Data, DOI: 10.17632/mgmxv5tjt2.1.
